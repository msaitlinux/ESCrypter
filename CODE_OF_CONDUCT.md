# Community conduct

Be respectful and discuss technical disagreements with evidence. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, doxxing and publishing another person's secrets are not
accepted. Review code rather than attacking contributors. Respect privacy when
sharing logs, sample files and screenshots.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation. Follow
GitHub's reporting mechanisms for platform abuse. To request a private channel for
project-specific concerns, contact a maintainer through the repository without
posting sensitive details publicly. No separate contact address has been configured.
